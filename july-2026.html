Accounting lens
Achieved substantial completion (construction essentially complete, capable of operating) and COD under the LLCA is imminent. LLCA COD is the placed in service date and triggers the beginning of depreciation, the vesting of ITCs, final funding by the TE partner, and the beginning of HLBV accounting.

Operations updates

Prospero II — term loan extension signed. The loan extension was executed on July 16th and extends the maturity date to 12/31/2026, giving the project time to contract the remaining open 80MW and refinance under improved terms.

Accounting lens
Completed the ASC 470-50 quantitative analysis (10% cash flow test) — the change falls short of the brightline threshold, so the restructuring is accounted for as a modification. No gain or loss is recognized on the modification itself; the carrying amount is adjusted for net lender fees, a revised effective interest rate of approximately 6.47% is applied prospectively, and third-party costs of ~$20.6K are expensed as incurred.

Crowe · July 2026
Wind and solar safe harbor survives court ruling
A June 6 federal court ruling vacated IRS Notice 2025-42, restoring the 5% safe harbor for beginning-of-construction determinations just ahead of the July 4 statutory deadline for §45Y/48E eligibility. The government is expected to appeal, so developers relying on the restored safe harbor for construction starts near the deadline carry real risk that it could be reinstated mid-litigation.

Enverus Intelligence Research · July 29, 2026
Race to resiliency: tax-credit phaseout expected to push renewable PPA prices higher
Enverus screened over 3,200 U.S. wind and solar projects with first-power dates before the end of 2027 and found roughly 760 (mostly solar) are dependent on IRA tax credits, meaning their levelized costs exceed projected merchant power and REC revenue once those credits phase out. Projects that miss the July 4 construction-start or 2027 in-service deadlines will need materially stronger PPA pricing to pencil, with hyperscalers likely more willing to absorb the premium than other offtakers.

Lazard · July 13, 2026
Renewables remain the lowest-cost new-build generation despite rising cost pressures
Lazard's 19th annual Levelized Cost of Energy+ report finds unsubsidized renewables still the cheapest new-build generation option, even as costs rise across every technology amid tariffs, supply-chain pressure, and unprecedented data-center-driven demand growth. Wind, solar, and storage are still expected to make up the majority of near-term U.S. capacity additions given their comparatively short deployment timelines — a trend directly relevant to how we underwrite new PPA pricing.

ASU updates
ASU 2025-10 · NEW ↗
Government Grants (Topic 832)
Issued July 7, 2026. FASB's first authoritative U.S. GAAP guidance for recognizing and measuring government grants received by business entities — previously handled by analogy to IAS 20. The ASU defines "government grant" and sets recognition/measurement requirements that vary by whether the grant relates to an asset or to income. Effective for PBEs in fiscal years beginning after Dec 15, 2029; one year later for other entities. Modified prospective, modified retrospective, or full retrospective transition permitted.
Where we landed
As it relates to transferrable tax credits, TA evaluating whether we can analogize to the new ASC 832 standard rather than ASC 740. No impact in 2026. Will be a 2027 change in policy, if elected.
ASU 2026-01 ↗
Initial Measurement of Paid-in-Kind Dividends (Topic 505)
Issued April 23, 2026. Requires PIK dividends on equity-classified preferred stock to be measured based on the stated PIK dividend rate in the preferred stock agreement, eliminating prior diversity in practice. Applies to all entities. Effective for annual periods beginning after Dec 15, 2026; early adoption permitted.
Where we landed
Deemed not applicable to Longroad.
ASU 2026-02 ↗
Environmental Credits and ECOs (Topic 818)
Issued May 19, 2026. Establishes the first comprehensive accounting model for environmental credits and environmental credit obligations. Entities recognize assets based on intended use (compliance vs. noncompliance vs. voluntary) and how credits are obtained. Effective for PBEs in fiscal years beginning after Dec 15, 2027; one year later for all others. Early adoption permitted.
Where we landed
We're effectively already there — our current REC/environmental credit treatment lines up with the ASC 818 model, so we will formally early-adopt the ASU, however, there is no change to our current accounting methodology.
