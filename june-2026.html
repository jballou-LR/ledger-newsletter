Energy IB · March 2026
The data center power boom: AI demand, hyperscaler offtake, and 50+ GW of incremental load
US data center power demand is projected to reach approximately 76 GW by 2026, up from roughly 50 GW in 2024. Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and Meta together accounted for roughly half of all global clean energy purchase agreements signed in 2025, and hyperscaler capex is forecast to exceed $600 billion in 2026. Corporate procurement has evolved beyond REC purchasing toward hourly matching — raising the bar on what offtake structures need to deliver, and making bankable hyperscaler PPAs among the most sought-after assets in project finance.

2026 Renewable Energy Industry Outlook: speed, resilience, and the OBBBA inflection point
Deloitte's annual outlook frames 2026 as a year defined by compressed timelines and supply chain pressure. FASB · May 19, 2026
FASB issues first-ever standard on environmental credits
ASU 2026-02 establishes comprehensive GAAP guidance for recognizing, measuring, and disclosing environmental credits and related obligations — RECs, carbon offsets, and compliance credits included. Previously, companies accounted for these through inventory, intangibles, or contingencies with wide variation. The new standard defines treatment based on intended use and how credits are obtained. White & Case / Utility Dive · June 2026
FERC orders all major grid operators to reform large-load interconnection rules
In June 2026, FERC issued show cause orders to all six jurisdictional RTOs — including PJM, MISO, and SPP — requiring them to revise or justify their interconnection rules for data centers and other large loads. The orders follow a DOE directive from October 2025 and focus on standardizing study deposits, accelerating queue timelines, and addressing cost allocation for network upgrades. PJM alone projects 30 GW of new data center demand by 2030. The action has direct implications for developers structuring behind-the-meter and co-location arrangements.

ASU updates
ASU 2025-10 ↗
Government Grants (Topic 832)
First explicit U.S. GAAP guidance on accounting for government grants — previously most entities defaulted to IAS 20 by analogy. Recognition requires conditions to be substantially met. Entities elect gross vs. net P&L presentation. Effective for annual periods beginning after Dec 15, 2026; nonpublic entities one year later. Early adoption permitted.

ASU 2025-12 ↗
Codification Improvements
Addresses 33 technical corrections and clarifications across a range of topics. Notable items include clarified diluted EPS calculation when a loss from continuing operations exists, permissible treasury stock retirement methods, and lease receivable disclosure requirements under ASC 842. Effective for all entities for annual periods beginning after Dec 15, 2026. Early adoption permitted.

ASU 2026-01 ↗
Initial Measurement of Paid-in-Kind Dividends (Topic 505)
Issued April 23, 2026. Requires PIK dividends on equity-classified preferred stock to be measured based on the stated PIK dividend rate in the preferred stock agreement — eliminating prior diversity in practice. Applies to all entities. Effective for annual periods beginning after Dec 15, 2026; early adoption permitted.

ASU 2026-02 · NEW ↗
Environmental Credits and ECOs (Topic 818)
Issued May 19, 2026. Establishes the first comprehensive accounting model for environmental credits and environmental credit obligations. Entities recognize assets based on intended use (compliance vs. noncompliance vs. voluntary) and how credits are obtained. Effective for PBEs in fiscal years beginning after Dec 15, 2027; one year later for all others. Early adoption permitted. The standard also adds environmental credit expense categories to the ASU 2024-03 income statement disaggregation disclosures.
